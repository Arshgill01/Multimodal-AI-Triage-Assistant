# Skill: Frontend HUD

Use this skill when editing the frontend.

## Style constraints

Preserve:
- dark Obsidian HUD aesthetic
- strong ESI color semantics
- hero-style single-patient output
- streaming intelligence panel
- MCI mode as a separate operational surface

Avoid:
- generic dashboard redesigns
- flattening the visual drama of the product
- removing motion or hierarchy without reason
- burying the prediction hero under too much detail

- `TelemetryPane.tsx`
- `AICorePane.tsx`
